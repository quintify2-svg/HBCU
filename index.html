HBCU Gallery

Introduction

What is an HBCU?

Historically Black Colleges and Universities are institutions founded to educate Black Americans when most colleges barred them from enrollment.

A lasting mission

Built for access

Most HBCUs began before 1964. They opened doors to higher learning, leadership, and professions that segregation had closed.

Legacy

Where leaders rise

From medicine and law to arts, science, and civil rights, HBCU alumni have shaped American culture, scholarship, and public life.
